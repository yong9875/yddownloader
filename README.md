# ydDownloader

A premium, open-source GUI video downloader powered by [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp).

## Features
- **Multi-Platform Support**: Download videos from YouTube, Bilibili, Twitter, and hundreds of other websites supported by yt-dlp.
- **Smart Playlist & Channel Downloads**: Automatically detects playlists and channel URLs to fetch and batch download multiple videos.
- **Subscription Auto-Download**: Subscribe to your favorite channels. ydDownloader will periodically check for new videos and prompt you to download them.
- **Subtitle Auto-Embedding**: Optionally automatically fetch and embed multi-language subtitles into your video files for seamless playback.
- **Bilibili High Quality**: Enter your Bilibili cookies to unlock premium 1080p+ resolutions.
- **Portable Mode**: Runs as a single portable application without requiring a complex installation.
